PromptBox — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Short version: PromptBox does not collect, transmit, or share any of your prompts or AI chats. Everything you save stays inside your own browser, on your own device.

1. What data PromptBox stores

PromptBox stores the following information locally in your browser using the chrome.storage.local API:

  • Your saved prompts (titles and text content)
  • Your folder/category names
  • Your license state (Free / Pro, license key, and activated device instance ID)

2. What data we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect browsing history, page content, or analytics telemetry.
  • We do not transmit your saved prompts to any server.
  • We do not read or transmit the content of your conversations with ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini. The extension only inserts text into the input box when you explicitly trigger it with the / shortcut.
  • We do not sell or share user data with third parties.

3. Data storage & retention

All prompt data lives in your browser profile via chrome.storage.local. It is never uploaded. If you uninstall PromptBox, or clear your browser data, all saved prompts are permanently deleted from your device. You can export your prompts as a JSON file at any time for backup.

4. Payments & License Verification

The optional Pro upgrade ($6.99 USD one-time purchase) is processed by our Merchant of Record, Creem (Armitage Labs OÜ), which collects standard billing and tax compliance details directly on its secure checkout page in accordance with Creem's own Privacy Policy. PromptBox never sees or stores your payment card details.

After checkout you receive a license key by email. To unlock Pro, manage device seats, or check license status, PromptBox sends only that license key, device label, and returned device instance ID to our Cloudflare Worker license verification endpoint, which communicates with Creem's official license API (/v1/licenses/activate, /validate, and /deactivate) using an encrypted server-side API key.

Your prompts, folders, and AI conversations never leave your device.
