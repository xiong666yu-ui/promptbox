PromptBox — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Short version: PromptBox does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data. Everything you save stays inside your own browser, on your own device.

1. What data PromptBox stores

PromptBox stores the following information locally in your browser using the chrome.storage.local API:

  • Your saved prompts (titles and text content)
  • Your folder/category names
  • Your license state (Free / Pro)

2. What data we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect personal information, browsing history, or page content.
  • We do not transmit your prompts to any third-party server. There is no backend, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind.
  • We do not read the content of your conversations with ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini. The extension only inserts text into the input box when you explicitly trigger it with the / shortcut.
  • We do not sell or share data with anyone — there is no data to sell.

3. Data storage & retention

All data lives in your browser profile via chrome.storage.local. It is never uploaded. If you uninstall PromptBox, or clear your browser data, all saved prompts are permanently deleted from your device. You can export your prompts as a JSON file at any time for backup.

4. Payments & License Verification

The optional Pro upgrade (one-time purchase) is processed by our payment provider Creem. PromptBox never sees or stores your payment details.

After checkout you receive a license key by email. To unlock Pro, PromptBox sends only that license key (plus a device name you can edit) to our own Cloudflare Worker endpoint (promptbox-license.workers.dev). The Worker forwards the key to Creem's license API using a server-side API key that is never exposed to your browser, and returns a device instance ID. PromptBox then stores the license key and that instance ID locally via chrome.storage.local. The plugin periodically re-validates the key through the same Worker so that a refunded or revoked license simply falls back to the Free tier.

Your prompts, folders and AI conversations never leave your device — only the license key used for activation is transmitted, and only to our Worker and Creem. We do not collect, sell, or share any personal data, chat history, or usage statistics.

5. Refund policy

Pro purchases come with a 7-day money-back guarantee. If you are not satisfied, email us within 7 days of purchase and we will issue a full refund — no questions asked.

  • Host access to chatgpt.com, claude.ai, gemini.google.com — required to show the / prompt menu inside the AI chat input box on these three sites. No content is read or sent anywhere.
